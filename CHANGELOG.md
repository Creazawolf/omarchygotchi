# Changelog

## Unreleased

- Hatching is a moment: knock, crack, and the baby climbs out of its shell. Click the egg or press Enter.
- Name your companion right after it hatches, and rename it by clicking its name.
- Agent buddy in the bar: a propeller cap while an agent works, a hop when it finishes, and a sign while it waits for you. Optional `cheer` and `waiting` commands for Claude Code hooks.
- The habitat notices theme switches.
- Halloween week: a witch hat and a pumpkin (setting: `seasonal`).
- A clearer home: one story, one next step, human durations, and consistent type.
- Calm motion setting for less movement.
- Play moved from `L` to `G`; `H`/`L` switch tabs and `J`/`K` scroll, like the shell's other panels.
- Fixes: shadows under young creatures, sparkles follow the creature, keepsake corners and a stray star, no "Add friend" for existing friends.

## 3.0.0 — 2026-09-15

First public release of Omarchygotchi for Omarchy.

- Visible visitors in the bar and shared habitat scenes.
- Persistent relationship memories, personality stories, gifts, and keepsakes.
- Optional mutual romance and shared eggs with explicit agreement on their home.
- Family albums, inherited appearance, and automatic child growth.
- PNG keepsakes with editable captions.
- Gentle care, healthy returns, and quieter defaults.
- Existing 2.x pet saves and community identities remain supported.

The public community server runs 3.0. Older servers retain their supported features through capability detection.
